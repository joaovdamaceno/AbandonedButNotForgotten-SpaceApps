# Pioneer 10 — Captions by minute (revised: British English, one continuous story)

**Voice.** Calm, warm and a little understated, like a good documentary narrator speaking to clever children. British spelling and dates (kilometres, colour, analyser, 4 December 1973). Each caption hands the thread to the next with a small link ("So", "But", "Then", "And yet"), and the same question runs underneath the whole film: *can anyone pass this way?*

**Timings** are approximate (~6:40) and serve as a pacing guide. Facts are unchanged from the timeline; only the writing has been reshaped.

## Writer's notes

- **Thread:** the opening line promises "those who go first"; each scene then answers one version of "can anyone pass this way?" (Mars, the belt, Jupiter's radiation, the years, the silence) and the finale pays it off with the six "firsts".
- **Link words** between captions (So, But, Then, Which left, And yet) are what turn separate facts into a single story. Keep them when cutting for time; cut a fact before cutting a link.
- **One claim removed:** the earlier line saying red light "shows lots of detail" was dropped, because it was not confirmed for Pioneer 10. The minigame captions now describe only the process, which is documented.
- **Reading time:** the busiest slots are 3:13–3:25 (radiation) and 4:43–4:58 (the years). If testing shows kids cannot finish reading, trim the second half of those captions first.
